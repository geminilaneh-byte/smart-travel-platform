# Travel Facts Verification

1. Prefer regulator, operator, bank/issuer and official marketplace sources.
2. Record URL, exact observation date, country, eligibility, documents, price/tax, validity and caveats.
3. A search snippet or affiliate blog is discovery only; open the official page before publishing.
4. Do not claim a bank/prepaid card is available to tourists unless issuer eligibility and KYC are explicit.
5. Do not call a discount real from advertised percentage; require price history or label it advertised.
6. Mark uncertain or region/branch-dependent facts clearly and set recheck date.
7. Never suggest sanctions evasion, identity borrowing, unlicensed exchange or anonymous financial products.
